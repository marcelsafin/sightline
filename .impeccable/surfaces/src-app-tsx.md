## Scope and mode

`src/App.tsx` is an Operate surface ported from the user-selected,
thumbnail-matching `Sightline Workbench v5.dc.html`.

## Audience, job, and proof

Developer-first product teams repair a page with a browser agent. Real proof
is fourteen axe barriers, an exact DOM target, a scoped patch, explicit human
approval, re-scan, undo, and score 72 to 100.

## Direction and memorable moment

An Apple-style light-gray workbench holds one solid white Stride for Life page
and one translucent review rail. Blue means agent action, orange means the
human must decide, and green means re-scan verified the change. The memorable
moment is the blue node outline plus a bottom approval sheet while the rail
says Your turn.

## Interaction and constraints

The rail owns status, progress, Watch the agent work, Issues, Agent log, Undo,
and Start over. Issues expand in place. The approval sheet remains attached to
the page. Preserve the real eight WebMCP tool surface, viewport-independent
14/72 scan, synthetic fixture labelling, and non-compliance claim.
